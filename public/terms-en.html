TERMS & CONDITIONS

Accommodation terms and conditions

Guests of SORABE ("the property") must agree to these terms and the conditions shown when booking.

1. Scope

These terms apply to lodging contracts with the property and related agreements. Where a booking platform has separate terms, those terms take precedence.

2. Applying for and confirming a reservation

When requesting a reservation, provide your dates, group size, contact details, and other information we require. A direct reservation is confirmed when we notify you that it is confirmed.

3. Rates and payment

The base rate is ¥50,000 per night for the entire cottage, including tax, cleaning, and bedding. Direct bookings are paid by bank transfer. Payment is due on the later of 10 days before check-in or the day after booking; guests pay transfer fees. We may cancel if payment is not received by the deadline. Booking platform rates and payment terms apply to reservations made there.

4. Check-in and check-out

Standard check-in is 15:00 and check-out is 10:00. Early check-in and late check-out each cost ¥5,000 per two hours including tax and require advance notice. Entry uses a digital key; we send the code after booking.

5. Cancellation

Cancellation is free up to 11 days before check-in, 50% from 10 to 5 days before, and 100% from 4 days before through check-in day. For booking platform reservations, the displayed terms take precedence.

6. Capacity and use of the property

Maximum capacity is 21 guests. Bedding for futons needed in addition to beds is included in the base rate. You may not exceed the booked guest count or admit unregistered visitors without permission. The property is entirely smoke-free and has no kitchen or cooking equipment.

7. Pets

In principle, one small dog is allowed for ¥5,500 including tax. Medium dogs are allowed only in one designated room. Use the provided crate and keep the dog inside it during the stay. We may charge actual repair or cleaning costs for pet-related damage or soiling.

8. Paid options and fire safety

The fire pit, BBQ grill, and smokeless roaster each cost ¥5,500 including tax; billiards costs ¥1,000. Only the fire pit or grill is provided. Guests must bring firewood, food, and charcoal. The smokeless roaster can be used in rainy weather. Completely extinguish and secure any fire after use.

9. Waste and prohibited conduct

Bag food waste and take it to the designated waste station. Do not leave it outdoors. Disturbing neighbors, dangerous conduct, misuse of facilities, and unlawful conduct are prohibited.

10. Damage and termination of use

If you intentionally or negligently soil, damage, or lose property or equipment, we may charge repair, replacement, or special cleaning costs. We may end a stay for a breach of these terms or a safety issue.

11. Safety and emergencies

Follow the in-house manual for operating equipment, evacuation, emergency contacts, and check-out procedures. Snow and ice are possible in winter, so please use winter tires.

12. Personal information

We handle personal information collected for bookings and stays according to our privacy policy.
